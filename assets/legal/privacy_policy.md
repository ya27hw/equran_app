# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

eQuran is built to respect your privacy. In short: **there are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking in the app, and the developers do not collect or receive your personal data.** The sections below explain what is stored on your device and which third-party servers the app contacts when you use particular features.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

The following stay on your device and are never sent to us:

- reading position, bookmarks, favourite ayahs and the notes you write;
- Hifz (memorisation) and reading-plan progress, statistics and goals;
- dua favourites, Tasbih counts, and app settings (theme, language, reciter, fonts, prayer-time method and so on);
- downloaded audio, translations, tafsir and fonts;
- the prayer location you choose or the last location the app detected.

If you use "Backup", the app writes a file to a location you choose. You decide where that file goes and who can access it. Uninstalling the app removes its internal data from your device.

## 2. Purchases

If you subscribe, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not see or store your name, payment method or Apple ID. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active so it can unlock the features. We do not use purchase information for advertising or tracking.

## 3. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | To stream or download recitations and optional resources, show map tiles, resolve place names, and fetch Zakat price data. |
| Location (approximate / precise) | To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. Only requested if you choose to use your current location. |
| Notifications and exact alarms | To deliver the prayer reminders you turn on. |
| Run at boot | To restore your prayer reminders after the device restarts. |
| Foreground service / wake lock | To keep audio playing when the screen is off. |

You can revoke any permission in your system settings; the related feature will then be unavailable.

## 4. Your location

Your device location is used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The app does not send your location to us.

To show a readable place name, the app may send your coordinates to a reverse-geocoding service: your operating system's own geocoder, and/or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Those services receive the coordinates and your IP address and are governed by their own policies. If you prefer, choose a city manually.

## 5. Third-party servers the app may contact

When you use the features below, your device connects directly to these services. They can see your IP address and the request itself, as with any internet request.

| Service | When | What it can see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| everyayah.com | Streaming or downloading recitations | The surah/ayah and reciter you request |
| GitHub (github.com, "equran-assets" releases) | Listing and downloading optional translations, tafsir, timings and fonts | The resource you request |
| OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org, nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | Prayer-location map and place-name lookup | The map area you view or the coordinates you look up |
| CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) | Zakat calculator, to fetch gold prices | The currency you selected |
| Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) | Displaying some interface and Arabic fonts that are fetched when first needed | Standard request data |
| quranapi.pages.dev | A lightweight connectivity check before audio playback | Standard request data |
| corsproxy.io | **Web version only**, as a fallback for audio playback | The audio URL requested |

We do not control these services. Please consult their own privacy policies.

## 6. Sharing

If you use the share feature (for example sharing an ayah as an image or text), the content is handed to the app you choose through your system's share sheet. We do not receive it.

## 7. Children

eQuran does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 8. Security

Data lives in your device's app storage and is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected and be careful where you save backups.

## 9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data held by the third-party services listed above is subject to their own policies and your rights against them.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
