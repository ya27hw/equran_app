## 4. Prayer times, Qibla, calendar and Zakat tools

- **Prayer times** are calculated on your device from your location and the calculation method you choose. Different methods and local conditions give different results. Times can be wrong, particularly at high latitudes or when your location, time zone or device clock is wrong. Confirm with your local mosque or authority when it matters, especially for fasting times.
- **Qibla direction** depends on your device's compass and location sensors, which can be inaccurate or disturbed by metal and magnetic fields. Treat it as an approximation and verify by other means when you can.
- **Hijri dates** are approximate and may differ from local moon-sighting decisions.
- **Reminders and notifications** rely on your operating system. Battery optimisation, silent modes, permissions and system restrictions can delay or prevent them. Do not rely on them for anything time-critical.
- **The Zakat calculator** gives an estimate only. Gold and silver prices are fetched from a third-party service and may be delayed, unavailable or inaccurate, and the silver price is approximated. Rulings on Zakat differ between schools and circumstances. Consult a qualified scholar before paying or withholding Zakat.
