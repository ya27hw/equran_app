## Religious content

eQuran is a reading and study aid, not a source of religious rulings. It does not replace a qualified scholar or teacher. If something matters for your worship, check with someone qualified.

## Accuracy of the Quran text

We have taken care with the Arabic text, but no digital copy is free from the possibility of error. For recitation, memorisation or teaching, always verify against a printed mushaf approved by a recognised authority. If you find a mistake, please tell us so we can correct it.

## Translations, transliteration and tafsir

These are human interpretations of the meanings of the Quran. They are not the Quran itself, they differ between authors, and they can contain mistakes. Some translations of supplications were produced with automated assistance and may be inaccurate.

## Prayer times, Qibla, calendar, reminders

These are calculated or sensed on your device and are approximate. Compasses can be disturbed, calculation methods differ, and notifications can be delayed by your system. Confirm with your local mosque or authority when it matters.

## Zakat calculator

An estimate only, based on figures you enter and third-party gold price data that may be delayed or inaccurate. Consult a qualified scholar before paying or withholding Zakat.
