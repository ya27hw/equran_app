# Religious content policy

Canonical Quran text, verse numbering, page mappings, translations, tafsir, hadith, and fiqh data are source-controlled or imported from a verified, licensed pack with stable identifiers and provenance. The app must distinguish Quran text, translation, tafsir, hadith, user reflection, and machine-generated organizational output.

The roadmap never generates, rewrites, “corrects,” or paraphrases Quran text; invents tafsir, hadith, rulings, or theological relationships; or implies scholarly authority. AI may analyze local memorization signals, rank approved references, explain numeric prediction inputs, or assist navigation. It may not independently create religious conclusions.

Every journey/tafsir/hadith item requires source, edition/grade where applicable, license, stable ID, review status, and pack version. Unreviewed or unlicensed packs stay disabled. Explanatory UI uses non-authoritative language and links the provenance. Quran navigation derives page/ayah relationships only from verified metadata.
